Help & quick start

Find a task, follow the steps, and get back to your inspection.

This guide is included with the offline app. Before going offline, open the app online and wait for “Ready for offline use”. If you opened Help from a form, close this tab to return to that form.

Quick start: your first inspection
  1. Select New inspection. Enter the title, date and inspector, then select Save inspection.
  2. Start with Take photo or Add images to capture evidence first. A finding is created and each readable original is saved immediately. Alternatively, select Add finding to enter details first.
  3. Enter or dictate the description, add location, category and priority, then select Save finding. Photo-first entries show previews. Use Finish later to keep unfinished details and photos; a finding without a description stays visibly incomplete. Closing the form also keeps captured photos but does not save form edits. Discard finding asks for confirmation before removing an incomplete entry.
  4. Open its summary. Use Take photo or Add images to add one or more photos. Select a photo to annotate it. Voice notes are optional audio attachments.
  5. Review the description, location, priority and photos, then select Mark reviewed. At least one photo is required to mark a finding reviewed.
  6. Select Export ZIP, choose a photo option and download. Keep a copy somewhere you can find later.

Check the local save status before leaving. A downloaded export is separate from the records saved in this browser.

Find, edit and review findings

Each inspection shows ten finding summaries per page. Use Previous and Next, or search by description, location, category or priority. Search covers the entire inspection, including other pages. Clear the search to see all findings.

Open a summary to see its photos, audio and editing controls. Opening another finding closes the previous one. A new finding opens on its own page. Search for “incomplete” to find entries needing a description or photos. An incomplete finding cannot be marked reviewed until it has a description and at least one photo.

Editing a finding or its attachments resets its review status. Review it again when ready. High, Medium and Low are inspection priority labels, not a formal risk assessment. Exports include all findings, regardless of the current search or page.

Take photos and edit annotations

From the inspection page, select Take photo or Add images to start a new finding with photos. Cancelling the picker or selecting only unreadable files does not create an empty finding. You can also open an existing finding and select Take photo to use the phone’s camera option, or Add images to select existing photos. Camera and file-picker options depend on your browser. JPEG and PNG are recommended if a camera format such as HEIC cannot be read.

  1. Select a photo to open its annotation workspace.
  2. Choose Arrow, Circle or Rectangle, then drag across the image.
  3. For Text, enter the text first, then tap the photo.
  4. To move a mark, choose it from the annotation list, select Move and drag. Use the text and colour controls to edit the selected mark.
  5. Use Delete selected or Undo last change as needed. To change a shape’s size, delete it and draw it again. Select Done to return.

Original photos remain unmarked. Editable annotations are stored separately. Export options determine whether original photos are included in the ZIP.

Dictate a description and save voice notes

To speak your finding description: tap Description, then use the microphone on your phone’s keyboard. Use on-device dictation where your device and language support it. Availability and local or online processing depend on your device settings. Check names, room numbers and safety details before saving.

To keep an audio recording: open a finding and select Voice note. Record audio or attach an existing audio file, review playback, then select Save audio note. Allow microphone access if asked when recording.

Recording stops after five minutes or when the app moves into the background. Save the note before leaving; cancelling an unsaved note discards it. Stop recording before using keyboard dictation.

These are separate actions: keyboard dictation enters description text; a voice note saves audio. The app has no automatic transcription engine or transcript entry field. Saved audio is included unchanged in every ZIP export option.

Understand local saving and offline readiness
  • Saving on this device… means a save is still in progress.
  • Saved on this device confirms local saving has completed.
  • Local save failed means changes may only be in memory. Export before closing and address the storage problem.
  • Ready for offline use means the app files are prepared for offline use. This is separate from saving your inspection records.
  • Network available reports connectivity; it does not confirm a cloud upload.

Drafts, original photos and audio stay in this browser on this device. Opening the app on another phone, browser or web address does not transfer them. Clearing browser data, private browsing or device storage policies can remove records. Keep exports regularly.

Before fieldwork, open the app online, wait for offline readiness, and check that your inspection opens. Export a copy after the inspection. Restore or import of exported ZIPs into this app is not available.

Choose an export option and organise the ZIP

From the inspection, select Export ZIP. Review any incomplete-findings warning, choose a photo option and download:

OptionIncluded photosUse when
Smaller archiveUnchanged originals and annotated JPEGs up to 1600 pixels.You want to retain originals while reducing the size of annotated copies.
Compact sharingAnnotated JPEGs up to 1600 pixels; no originals.You need a smaller sharing copy. It is not a full archive.
Full qualityUnchanged originals and annotated PNGs at original resolution.Fine image detail needs to be retained. Files can be much larger.

All options include finding metadata and saved audio. Findings with missing descriptions or photos remain in the export and are labelled incomplete in the report, CSV and JSON. Audio is unchanged and may increase the ZIP size.

  1. Find the downloaded ZIP in your browser’s downloads or your device’s Files app.
  2. Extract the entire ZIP. Open START-HERE.html for the inspection report.
  3. Keep the extracted folder structure together so report links to photos and audio work.

The export folder name includes the inspection date, title and a short record ID. Finding folders contain the finding record and associated files. findings.csv, photos.csv and audio.csv are spreadsheet registers; inspection.json contains structured records and editable annotation data.

Import floor, room and ID columns as text in spreadsheet software to retain leading zeroes. Finding references such as F001 reflect export order; use full record IDs to match records across exports.

The app does not upload the ZIP or import it back as a draft. Move the downloaded file to your agreed storage destination yourself.

Delete inspections and manage device storage

To remove one inspection, open it and select Delete inspection. To remove all local inspections, open Storage & export and select Clear local inspections & photos. Bulk clearing requires typing DELETE.

Export any records you need first. Deletion removes the selected local records, their photos, annotations and voice notes. It cannot be undone in the app. Close other app tabs before deleting to avoid stale records being saved again.

Downloaded exports and cloud files are unaffected. The offline app files are retained. The storage page also shows approximate browser storage use and offers a request for persistent device storage; the browser decides whether to grant it.

Install the app and apply updates

Installation is optional. A supported browser can use the prepared app offline.

  • iPhone or iPad: open the app in Safari, select Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android or desktop: select Install app if offered, or use the browser’s installation menu.

Open the app online once and wait for Ready for offline use. Finish recording and save your work before selecting Update & reload when a new version is offered. Keep an export copy of important records.

Troubleshoot photos, microphone, downloads and saving
The microphone will not start
Check microphone permission for this site in browser/device settings. Use the HTTPS app. If recording is unavailable, attach an existing audio file instead.
There is no keyboard dictation microphone
Check the device’s keyboard and dictation settings and language availability, or type the description. The app does not control the keyboard’s dictation feature.
A photo cannot be read
Try JPEG or PNG. Large or unsupported camera files may fail to decode on your phone.
A local save fails
Keep the page open and export the inspection before closing. Check free device space and browser storage permissions. Remove unneeded records only after exporting anything you need.
The ZIP is large or export fails
Try Compact sharing if you do not need originals in that copy. Audio still contributes to the size. Export needs working memory; very large inspections may exceed a phone’s capacity. Keep records until you have a usable export.
The HTML report has missing images or audio
Extract the whole ZIP before opening START-HERE.html. Keep associated folders alongside the report. Audio playback support varies; try another compatible browser/player for the saved audio file.
Offline readiness is unavailable
Reopen online using the HTTPS app and allow browser storage. Private browsing and device policy may restrict offline storage. Keep exports before clearing any browser data.
My records are missing on another device
Records are local to the browser and web address used to create them. Return to the original device/browser. There is no automatic synchronisation.
Dropbox, SharePoint and shared university storage

Cloud account connections and uploads are not implemented in this version. Signing into another service or having network connectivity does not mean an inspection has been uploaded.

Download a ZIP and place it in your agreed shared destination. Personal storage does not automatically provide central university storage. Future SharePoint connections may require administrator approval; Teams channel files use the underlying SharePoint document library.
